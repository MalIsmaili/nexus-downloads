What it does

One app for everything your PC needs.

A phone app and a small PC app. They pair with a QR code and stay linked, on your Wi-Fi or across the world.

Wake it from anywhere

Turn your PC on over mobile data. A spare phone at home acts as the Wake Relay, so it works even when the PC is fully shut down.

Locks when you leave

Walk away from your desk or leave the house, and the PC locks within seconds. Come home and it wakes up for you.

Full remote control

Touchpad, keyboard, media keys, volume per app, brightness, and closing frozen apps.

Presentation remote

Next and back, laser pointer, black screen and a timer. Your phone becomes the clicker.

Files both ways

Private by design

Only your phone can control your PC.

Every device has its own key. Our server passes messages along but can't read them or send commands in your name.

End-to-end encrypted

Phone and PC talk over a channel the server can't open.

Paired devices only

Keys are fixed at pairing, so no one can pretend to be your phone.

Remote wipe

PC stolen? Lock it and delete your private folders from your phone, with a 60-second countdown.

You stay in charge

Incoming files ask first. Unpair a device with one tap.

For schools

NEXUS Lab for a classroom.

One download for every PC in the room. Setup asks whether the PC is the teacher's or a student's.

NEXUS Lab

Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · 85 MB · v0.5.8
TEACHER + STUDENTS
  1. On the teacher PC, open NEXUS-Lab-Setup-0.5.8.exe and choose Teacher. NEXUS shows the lab code.
  2. On every student PC, choose Student, type the lab code and set an administrator PIN.
  3. The teacher sees every screen, runs the class and switches the room on and off. Students cannot stop sharing or uninstall without the PIN.
Download Lab

Setup needs an administrator. Student PCs must use standard (non-administrator) Windows accounts. Setting up many PCs: NEXUS-Lab-Setup.exe /VERYSILENT /ROLE=student /CODE=… /PIN=…
